# One-time neutral study continuation

This entry handles one explicitly approved continuation of the frozen nine-task
study. It does not restart the old execution, repair old answers or turn missing
identity into verified identity. The original claim, failed/pending cells,
request receipts and fees remain unchanged. It does not read custodian scoring,
gold or private qualification data.

The old segment processed three logical cells using seven requests and
$0.008200 conservative local accrual. All usage is settled, but request seven
has no retained verifiable fingerprint value/type. Its presence-only diagnostic
cannot establish uninterrupted identity. Continuing requires explicit acceptance
of that historical gap and the version/segment limitation. The provider
identity-retention fix applies only to new responses.

The continuation is restricted to the original **33 not-attempted logical
cells**, in their original order. No processed cell is eligible, including the
two unused call allocations from a failed cell. At most **99 new requests**
remain eligible, within the original cumulative **108-request/$2** limits.
The residual local spending allowance is **$1.991800**. Seven old plus 99 new
would total at most 106 requests; the unused two do not become retries. The
separate older experiment's 67 requests and $0.66 unknown holds are not inputs
or funds for this continuation.

## Preparation and consent boundary

`deepseek_study_continuation` prepares a new implementation-bound control from
the exact frozen source snapshot and unchanged neutral package. Old logical
cell IDs map to new execution IDs by task and strategy; both the mapping and
the ordered remaining list are frozen. Prompts, model/endpoint, evidence rules,
fixed plans, call/token/time limits and read scope remain unchanged.

The source reader accepts only files listed by the pinned terminal artifact
manifest. It verifies hashes before parsing metadata, known ledger totals,
closed results, unattempted cells and six retained canonical fingerprint values.
It does not discover or open later score files. An opaque custodian commitment
is carried as metadata; no gold file is opened to recompute it.

The proposal is unauthorized by default. A separate exact-scope approval must
bind the new profile, old snapshot, remaining cells, opening money/requests,
strict future pin and historical-gap acknowledgement. Copying or editing a
control does not create a new authorization. Approval records describe operator
consent, not cryptographic signatures or OS isolation.

At execution, a source-specific successor claim outside the original frozen
directory prevents another prepared directory from consuming the same remainder.
A permanent local execution claim is also required before Key ingress. Neither
claim is reset or removed by this entry. There is no automatic resume or retry.

## Request, cost and identity rules

Every new request persists the existing $0.33 reservation before dispatch.
The gate checks both the 99-request segment ceiling and cumulative original
limits, including old known spending and pending/new unknown reservations.
Known usage is settled independently of answer parsing. Missing/untrustworthy
usage retains its reservation and stops the segment. The local budget is not a
supplier billing hard cap or a guarantee the remainder will finish.

Future responses must match the actual fingerprint retained in the six verified
old responses. The gate does not reset its pin to a different new value. Missing,
invalid, mismatched or changed identity stops even when usage is known. A known
content-contract failure may be counted and advance only with independently
valid billing and matching identity. The old unverifiable response remains
unverified regardless of later matches.

Credential ingress remains the reviewed injected reader after exclusive claims;
only official DeepSeek authentication is authorized. Source tools remain scoped
to manifest-authorized neutral files. Logs stay local, owner-private, with the
existing 14-day/no-upload/no-automatic-delete policy. Host tools and same-user
paths are not OS-enforced gold isolation.

## Reporting and review

The segment summary distinguishes its 33 planned cells and new costs/requests
from the original 36-cell plan and opening totals. A combined report retains all
36 logical cells, original failures/pending outputs, new unattempted slots after
any halt, implementation versions, and the historical identity gap. It cannot
claim all requests share a verified uninterrupted identity or treat unavailable
pairs as observed ties.

Anonymous packets and keeper mappings remain separate. Only packets go to the
blind reviewer; the custodian retains mappings and merges gold after review
lock. No semantic scores are imported during unfinished generation. Contract
acceptance is not correctness, and mixed segment data alone cannot establish a
tool benefit.

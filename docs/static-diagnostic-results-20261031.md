# Static evidence comparison: a limited signal for continued research

Run: 2026-10-31. Report: 2026-11-01.

This nine-task synthetic paired study met its frozen engineering gate for
continuing static-evidence research. The evidence arm earned two semantic paired
wins, no losses and seven ties. This supports another controlled evaluation;
it does not establish general superiority, production reliability or an
orchestration benefit.

Direct API (R) and static evidence (E) received the same complete source,
question and assumptions. Both used `deepseek-flash`, thinking high, the
compact-v3 output contract and one-request allowance. E additionally
received a deterministic static capsule computed from that source. No target
code was executed and no external runtime evidence was added. The implementation
was fixed at [9492e87](https://github.com/Oliveryfaso/multi-agent-code-review-lab/commit/9492e87188aac9e7375fa6b9bcbb841d2eeb0075).
See the [frozen stage protocol](static-diagnostic-stage.md).

All 18 quality outputs were contract-valid and independently reviewed. They are
nine task observations, not 18 independent samples. All nine pairs were
comparable. The seven ties comprise two pairs with complete solutions on both
sides and five pairs with partial answers on both sides; ties do not establish
equivalent explanations or complete correctness. Correct verdicts alone earned
no full-solution credit.

The frozen nonexclusive error annotations recorded incomplete decisive arguments
in seven direct and five evidence outputs, and insufficient source support in
three outputs per arm. These tags overlap and must not be summed as independent
errors. Both arms still earned no complete necessary-abstention credit. This
does not mean all six gap outputs omitted a question: the aggregate annotations
recorded no missing decisive-evidence-request tag. Their remaining explanation
and support deficits matter. No false-positive, false-release or unsupported
determinate error was recorded; that does not make partial answers correct,
and the determinate-error bit is required to be false for abstentions.

E added 22,030 input tokens and $0.006043 in local accrual, a 23.2% cost increase.
Its nine capsules occupied 60,058 bytes and its additional preprocessing took
0.089098 seconds. Request-body JSON grew from 42,612 to 109,401 bytes; these are
serialized JSON counts, not raw HTTP captures or token estimates. The cost stayed
within the frozen 25% allowance. The single-run elapsed difference is an
observation, not a demonstrated speed advantage.

S1 used 18 requests and $0.058091 in local accrual. The separate six-request S0
development gate used $0.007827 and contributes no quality samples. The complete
stage used 24 real requests and $0.065918, with no unknown or in-flight item.
These amounts use credible usage/cache receipts and frozen peak rates; the
supplier invoice was not queried. Historical unknown reservations remain held.

The native decision was `advance_evidence_research`: net paired wins reached
two, no added unsupported determinate error was recorded, and the frozen cost,
time and delivery checks passed. It is a limited research decision. The
evaluation has only nine synthetic tasks and one response per arm. There is no
statistical-significance or general percentage-improvement claim.

Three fresh source-only panels locked judgments before three frozen-rubric
panels. Eleven of 18 outputs had substantive completeness disagreements that
were boundedly arbitrated; none remained unresolved. One arbitration panel
saw prior opinions before forming its provisional judgment. This order bias is
retained as a limitation. Identities, costs, order and effects were withheld
until keeper association, and source-only raters did not receive the capsules;
answer content may still reveal strategy. Shared assistant ecology and
same-account role separation do not constitute OS isolation, complete blinding
or absence of training contamination.

The post-lock audit also recorded 24 offline provider-object constructions and
request encodings during a read-only load, beyond that check's earlier
zero-object restriction. They made no network, Key or model calls and added no
observations; the final native aggregation itself made no provider calls.

The next research priority is complete decisive arguments and original-source
support, including adequate explanations of necessary abstention. It should use
public development fixtures and a new explicit profile if changed; this locked
study must not be repaired, rescored or reused for prompt tuning. No new paid
run or default product change follows from this result.

All result, review and determinate-review bindings are locked. The executor
verified anonymous bindings and imported custody's native sanitized aggregate;
private gold, task-category/qualification mappings and detailed custody
adjudication remain in custody. The execution owner's existing review-to-cell
keeper mapping is excluded from this report and was withheld from source-only
raters before their locks. Raw outputs, new task answers and private logs are
excluded from this report. Historical studies and locked scores are unchanged.
